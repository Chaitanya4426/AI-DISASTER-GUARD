Flood response guide

When flood risk rises, prioritize safety

01

Follow verified warnings

Monitor local authorities and emergency services. This app does not send SMS alerts.

02

Move to a verified safe place

Use evacuation routes and shelters designated by local authorities; never enter floodwater.

03

Prepare essential supplies

Keep drinking water, food, medicines, important documents, and a charged phone ready.

04

Report damage to responders

After it is safe, report blocked roads and power or communication outages to the responsible agency.
